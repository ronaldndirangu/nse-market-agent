## Method

- **Screen:** average turnover of at least KES 1,000,000 per session, at least 3 sourced annual periods, positive EPS, not suspended, price no older than 7 days.
- **Models:** banks use justified P/B = BVPS x (ROE - g) / (ke - g). Dividend payers use Gordon V = DPS x (1 + g) / (ke - g). Every counter gets earnings power value (3-year average EPS / ke), and sector median P/E and P/B when at least 3 peers have them. The Graham number is shown for reference only.
- **Cost of equity:** ke = (10-year bond - default spread) + beta x 4.23% + country risk premium 9.71%. Beta is a Blume-adjusted weekly beta against an equal-weight index of the universe.
- **Growth:** g = average ROE x retention, clipped to 0% to 7% and kept 2% below ke.
- **Undervalued:** at least max(2, half) of the models show a margin of safety of 15% or more, all screens pass and the quality gate passes.
- **Projected return:** net dividend yield + (1 + g) x (V / P)^(1/3) - 1 - round-trip costs spread over the horizon. Bear uses the low fair value and g - 2pp; bull uses the high fair value and g + 2pp.
- **Quality gate:** Piotroski F-score share, Altman Z'' (non-financials), bank NPL trend and profit-warning keywords in NSE announcements over the last 180 days.
- **AI:** No AI is used anywhere in this pipeline. Screening, valuation, quality checks, projected returns, ranking and this text all come from deterministic code and fixed templates.
